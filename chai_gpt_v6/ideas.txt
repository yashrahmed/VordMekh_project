--- Grok's High Level actions ------


- Peel and grate ginger (includes holding, discarding remnants; Masala-specific).
- Measure grated ginger with a teaspoon (Masala-specific).
- Measure ground cinnamon with a teaspoon (Masala-specific).
- Measure tea with a teaspoon (Loose Black Tea in Masala; Kashmiri Green Tea Leaves in Kashmiri).
- Measure milk with a cup.
- Measure water with a cup.
- Measure jaggery with a teaspoon (Masala-specific).
- Measure and add baking soda (Kashmiri-specific).
- Measure ice with a cup (Kashmiri-specific).
- Measure salt with a teaspoon (Kashmiri-specific).
- Measure and garnish with crushed nuts (Kashmiri-specific).
- Crush spices (e.g., cardamom pods and others in Masala; green cardamom pods in Kashmiri).
- Measure and add water to the pot.
- Add prepared spices/cardamom to the pot/mixture.
- Boil mixture and reduce to simmer (includes waiting for boiling, time elapsed, or liquid reduction).
- Reduce liquid by half through simmering (Kashmiri-specific).
- Add ice to the reduced mixture (Kashmiri-specific).
- Aerate Kashmiri tea mixture (after adding ice).
- Add tea to the mixture.
- Add milk to the mixture (and heat to just before boiling or until pink in Kashmiri).
- Simmer milk mixture until pink (Kashmiri-specific).
- Add salt to the tea (Kashmiri-specific).
- Add and stir jaggery until dissolved (Masala-specific).
- Stir the mixture (after adding various ingredients).
- Set up and ignite stove/heat source (includes moving pot, setting heat levels like High/Low/Medium).
- Turn off stove and remove pot (using mitts if no handle).
- Strain and pour into cups for serving (using Strainer or Muslin Cloth).
- Hold and release tools (e.g., spoon for stirring, pot with mitts).
- Discard waste (residues during cleanup).
