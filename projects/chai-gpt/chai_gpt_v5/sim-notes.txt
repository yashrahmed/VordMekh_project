High level plan -
	•	Peel and finely grate about ½ teaspoon of fresh ginger from a raw chunk.
	•	Lightly crush the cardamom pods, cloves, peppercorns, and fennel seeds together in a mortar and pestle.
	•	In a medium pot, add the grated ginger, crushed spices, ground cinnamon, and ¾ cup water. Bring to a boil, then cover and simmer for 5–6 minutes.
	•	Stir in 1 teaspoon loose black tea and simmer another 2–3 minutes.
	•	Pour in ½ cup whole milk and heat until just before boiling.
	•	Strain into a cup and stir in jaggery or sugar to taste.
