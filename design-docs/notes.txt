    The entire frame assumes:
        •	a surface exists (ground, cooktop),
        •	fuel-based or electric heat sources are plausible,
        •	the environment is static (you stand, cook, manipulate utensils).

    In a skydiving context:
        •	There’s no stable surface,
        •	No containment for heat (flame extinguished instantly),
        •	No usable gravity reference (so “up”, “down”, “pour”, “place” lose meaning).

    Hence, every attribute either becomes meaningless or underspecified.
