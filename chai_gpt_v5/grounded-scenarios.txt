Masala Chai — grounded action sequence  
Scenario - Chai preparation at home and the pot does not have a handle and cannot be handled with bare hands.

Prep aromatics  
1. Locate(Ginger Root).  
2. Hold(Ginger Root).  
3. Locate(Peeler).  
4. Peel(Ginger Root, Peeler).  
5. Locate(Grater).  
6. Grate(Ginger Root, Grater).  
7. Release(Ginger Root).  
8. Discard(Ginger Root Remnant, TrashContainer).  
9. Locate(Teaspoon).  
10. Measure(Grated Ginger, 0.5, tsp, Teaspoon).  
11. Locate(Cardamom Pods).  
12. Locate(Cloves).  
13. Locate(Peppercorns).  
14. Locate(Fennel Seeds).  
15. Locate(Mortar).  
16. Locate(Pestle).  
17. Crush([Cardamom Pods, Cloves, Peppercorns, Fennel Seeds], Mortar and Pestle).  

Build base in pot  
18. Locate(Pot).  
19. Locate(Stove).  
20. Move(Pot, Stove).  
21. TurnOn(Stove).  
22. SetHeat(Stove, High).  
23. Locate(Measuring Cup).  
24. Locate(Water).  
25. Measure(Water, 0.75, cup, Measuring Cup).  
26. Transfer(Water, Measuring Cup, Pot).  
27. Transfer(Grated Ginger, Teaspoon, Pot).  
28. Transfer(Crushed Spices, Mortar, Pot).  
29. Locate(Ground Cinnamon).  
30. Locate(Cinnamon Jar).  
31. Measure(Ground Cinnamon, 0.25, tsp, Teaspoon).  
32. Transfer(Ground Cinnamon, Teaspoon, Pot).  
33. Locate(Spoon).  
34. Hold(Spoon).  
35. Stir(Pot, Spoon).  
36. WaitUntil(Boiling).  
37. SetHeat(Stove, Low).  
38. WaitUntil(TimeElapsed(5m)).  

Tea step  
39. Locate(Loose Black Tea).  
40. Measure(Loose Black Tea, 1, tsp, Teaspoon).  
41. Transfer(Loose Black Tea, Teaspoon, Pot).  
42. Stir(Pot, Spoon).  
43. WaitUntil(TimeElapsed(2m)).  

Milk heat  
44. Locate(Whole Milk).  
45. Measure(Whole Milk, 0.5, cup, Measuring Cup).  
46. Transfer(Whole Milk, Measuring Cup, Pot).  
47. SetHeat(Stove, Medium).  
48. WaitUntil(JustBeforeBoiling).  
49. TurnOff(Stove).  

Strain & sweeten  
50. Locate(Mitts).  
51. Hold(Pot, Mitts).  
52. Move(Pot, Countertop).  
53. Locate(Strainer).  
54. Hold(Strainer).  
55. Locate(Cup).  
56. Strain(Pot, Strainer, Cup).  
57. Release(Strainer).  
58. Release(Pot).  
59. Locate(Jaggery).  
60. Locate(Jaggery Container).  
61. Measure(Jaggery, 1, tsp, Teaspoon).  
62. Transfer(Jaggery, Teaspoon, Cup).  
63. Stir(Cup, Spoon).  
64. Release(Spoon).  
65. WaitUntil(SweetenerDissolved).  

Cleanup  
66. Locate(TrashContainer).  
67. Hold(Strainer).  
68. Discard(Residues In Strainer, TrashContainer).  
69. Release(Strainer).
