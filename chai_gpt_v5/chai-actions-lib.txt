Chai action library - 

Object location & control
	•	Locate(X) — find/identify object X so it can be used next.
	•	Hold(X) — grasp/stabilize X with bare hands for precise manipulation.
	•	Hold(X, Tool) — grasp/stabilize X using Tool (mitts, towel, tongs) when heat/slip risk exists.
	•	Release(X) — cease holding X.
	•	Move(X, Y) — relocate intact solid X to location Y (pots, lids, tools, cups). Not for pourables.

Measuring & portioning
	•	Measure(Substance, Quantity, Unit, Vessel) — portion Substance into Vessel with the specified amount (does not deposit into the final container yet).

Substance vs. solid movement
	•	Transfer(Substance, Source, Target) — move pourable/scoopable matter (liquid, powder, grated items, leaves) from Source to Target.
Disambiguation: use Move for intact solids; use Transfer for anything that flows or is a portioned mass.

Ingredient preparation
	•	Peel(Object, Tool) — remove outer layer with Tool.
	•	Grate(Object, Tool) — reduce to small particles with Tool.
	•	Crush([Object], Tool) — crack/break spices or similar with Tool.

Mixing & agitation
	•	Stir(Container, Tool) — low-shear mixing; distribute contents with a tool.
	•	Whisk(Container, Tool) — rapid, continuous tool-driven mixing to entrain air (turbulence created by the tool inside the liquid).
	•	Aerate(Container, Tool) — entrain air by lifting/pouring the liquid (e.g., with a ladle) so it falls back in from a height; air added via motion between container and air.

Heat control (simplified)
	•	TurnOn(Stove) — supply heat to burner.
	•	TurnOff(Stove) — cut heat to burner.
	•	SetHeat(Stove, Level) — set burner to low/medium/high (or equivalent discrete levels).

Timing & conditions
	•	WaitUntil(Condition) — pause until a condition holds.
Examples: 5 minutes elapsed, Boiling, LiquidReducedByHalf, SteepTime(3m).

Separation, disposal, finishing
	•	Strain(Source, Strainer, Target) — pass liquid from Source through Strainer into Target to remove solids.
	•	Discard(Object, TrashContainer) — place unwanted Object into a TrashContainer (explicit disposal).
	•	Garnish(Target, Ingredient) — place finishing Ingredient onto/into Target without mixing (visual/texture accent; not stirred in).

Masala Chai — grounded action sequence (pre-dawn, no light source)  
Scenario - (Chai in the Dark at 4:30 am before sunrise with Butane Stove, pot without handles, and Camping Lantern; limited visibility requires stable lighting from lantern for every Locate and Transfer, with slower, deliberate movements).

Setup lighting and campsite  
1. Locate(Camping Lantern).  
2. TurnOn(Camping Lantern).  
3. Locate(Butane Stove).  
4. Locate(Pot).  
5. Move(Pot, Butane Stove).  
6. Locate(Butane Canister).  
7. Hold(Butane Stove).  
8. Move(Butane Canister, Butane Stove).  
9. Release(Butane Stove).  
10. TurnOn(Butane Stove).  
11. SetHeat(Butane Stove, High).  

Prep aromatics (under lantern light)  
12. Locate(Ginger Root).  
13. Hold(Ginger Root).  
14. Locate(Peeler).  
15. Peel(Ginger Root, Peeler).  
16. Locate(Grater).  
17. Grate(Ginger Root, Grater).  
18. Release(Ginger Root).  
19. Discard(Ginger Root Remnant, TrashContainer).  
20. Locate(Teaspoon).  
21. Measure(Grated Ginger, 0.5, tsp, Teaspoon).  
22. Locate(Cardamom Pods).  
23. Locate(Cloves).  
24. Locate(Peppercorns).  
25. Locate(Fennel Seeds).  
26. Locate(Mortar).  
27. Locate(Pestle).  
28. Crush([Cardamom Pods, Cloves, Peppercorns, Fennel Seeds], Mortar and Pestle).  

Build base in pot  
29. Locate(Water).  
30. Locate(Measuring Cup).  
31. Measure(Water, 0.75, cup, Measuring Cup).  
32. Transfer(Water, Measuring Cup, Pot).  
33. Transfer(Grated Ginger, Teaspoon, Pot).  
34. Transfer(Crushed Spices, Mortar, Pot).  
35. Locate(Ground Cinnamon).  
36. Locate(Cinnamon Jar).  
37. Measure(Ground Cinnamon, 0.25, tsp, Teaspoon).  
38. Transfer(Ground Cinnamon, Teaspoon, Pot).  
39. Locate(Spoon).  
40. Hold(Spoon).  
41. Stir(Pot, Spoon).  
42. WaitUntil(Boiling).  
43. SetHeat(Butane Stove, Low).  
44. WaitUntil(TimeElapsed(5m)).  

Tea steep  
45. Locate(Loose Black Tea).  
46. Measure(Loose Black Tea, 1, tsp, Teaspoon).  
47. Transfer(Loose Black Tea, Teaspoon, Pot).  
48. Stir(Pot, Spoon).  
49. WaitUntil(TimeElapsed(2m)).  

Milk heat  
50. Locate(Whole Milk).  
51. Measure(Whole Milk, 0.5, cup, Measuring Cup).  
52. Transfer(Whole Milk, Measuring Cup, Pot).  
53. SetHeat(Butane Stove, Medium).  
54. WaitUntil(JustBeforeBoiling).  
55. TurnOff(Butane Stove).  

Strain & sweeten (with pot mitts)  
56. Locate(Mitts).  
57. Hold(Pot, Mitts).  
58. Move(Pot, Campsite Table).  
59. Locate(Strainer).  
60. Hold(Strainer).  
61. Locate(Cup).  
62. Strain(Pot, Strainer, Cup).  
63. Release(Strainer).  
64. Release(Pot).  
65. Locate(Jaggery).  
66. Locate(Jaggery Container).  
67. Measure(Jaggery, 1, tsp, Teaspoon).  
68. Transfer(Jaggery, Teaspoon, Cup).  
69. Stir(Cup, Spoon).  
70. Release(Spoon).  
71. WaitUntil(SweetenerDissolved).  

Cleanup  
72. Locate(TrashContainer).  
73. Hold(Strainer).  
74. Discard(Residues In Strainer, TrashContainer).  
75. Release(Strainer).  
76. TurnOff(Camping Lantern).
