Introspection on how I add details -
	•	Pick a step in the plan.
	•	Read all previous steps.
	•	Pick a plausible scene for this step while taking into account all previous steps.
	•	Infer a set of objects involved in that step from the scene context. This step could result in multiple sets of objects.
		-	How can I do this without a world foundation model? ***
	•	Add extra steps to account for the interactions of some of the objects from the previous step with other objects in the scene.
		-	How can I do this without a world foundation model? ***
	•	Use the previous two steps to 
		-	add extra steps to account for objects not mentioned in the step.
	•	Use a parts graph to 
		-	add extra steps to account for object part.	
		-	add extra steps to account for some possible interaction of the parts with other objects in the scene (or their parts).
			-	How can I do this without a world foundation model? ***

Notes from GPT 5's critique - 
•	Use affordance libraries: tiny lists of “verb → typical tools/objects.” (“Grate → grater,” “boil → pot + heat source,” etc.)
•	Use frame semantics: a frame like “Cooking” expects roles like Agent, Ingredient, Tool, Heat Source. Fill them in from the current scene.
•	Use commonsense templates: not learned from scratch, just baked-in: “container needs contents,” “tool requires agent to operate,” “heat source has control.
