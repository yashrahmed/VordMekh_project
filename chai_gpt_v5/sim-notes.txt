Introspection on how I add details -
	•	Pick a step in the plan.
	•	Read all previous steps.
	•	Make a list of plausible scenes/frames with objects and constraints for this step while taking into account all previous steps.
		- I take into account different parts of objects and how they interact with other objects in the scene.
		- How different variants of objects would interact with each other in the scene or behave in the conditions in the scene.
	•	Now the problem becomes a problem of automatically generating this frame if we want to rely on LLMs to generate it.
	
Example of expanding a step using different frame/decompositions - 
Goal - 	
	•	Move the cooking pot to the top of the stove.

Plausible frames/decompositions -
	•	Frame with a person, a cooking pot being moved from one location to the top of the stove.
	•	Frame with a person, a cooking pot being moved from one location to the top of the stove but the pot must fit on the burner.
	•	Frame with a person, a cooking pot being moved from one location to the top of the stove but the stove top has to be cleared.
	•	Frame with a person, a cooking pot being moved from one location to the top of the stove but the stove needed to be clean first.
	•	Frame with a person, a cooking pot being moved from one location to the top of the stove but he needs to get a ladder to fetch the pot from the top shelf.



Notes from GPT 5's critique - 
•	Use affordance libraries: tiny lists of “verb → typical tools/objects.” (“Grate → grater,” “boil → pot + heat source,” etc.)
•	Use frame semantics: a frame like “Cooking” expects roles like Agent, Ingredient, Tool, Heat Source. Fill them in from the current scene.
•	Use commonsense templates: not learned from scratch, just baked-in: “container needs contents,” “tool requires agent to operate,” “heat source has control.
