
1. Tourné knife
    Name: Tourné knife
    Description: Tournée is the French word for "turned." It refers to a special vegetable cut that results in a 7-sided oblong shape. Some consider it a type of paring knife.
    actions:
        peel_round_produce
        extract_stem_core
        remove_surface_blemish
        sculpt_decorative_shape

2. Paring knife
    Name: Paring knife
    Description: A small, short-bladed knife (usually 3-4 inches) with a straight or slightly convex edge. It is the most versatile tool for precision tasks requiring control, usable both in the hand and on a cutting board.
    actions:
        peel_round_produce
        peel_irregular_produce
        extract_stem_core
        slice_small_produce
        mince_small_aromatics
        trim_meat_fat
        pierce_check_texture
        sever_binding_material

3. Slicer knife
    Name: Slicer knife
    Description: Long blade with a round or pointed tip; the blade may be flexible or rigid, tapered or round, or have a fluted edge.
    actions:
        carve_cooked_protein
        slice_cured_fish
        slice_uniform_portions
        portion_boneless_meat

4. Boning knife
    Name: Boning knife
    Description: Blade is thinner than that of a chef's knife, about 6 inches long, and rigid. It is used for separating raw meat from the bone.

        debone_raw_protein
        disjoint_carcass

5. Mandoline
    Name: Mandoline Slicer
    Description: Features blades of high-carbon steel. Levers adjust blades to achieve the cut and thickness desired. A guard provides safety. Used for slicing, julienning, and cutting gaufrettes and batonnets.
    actions:
        slice_uniform_produce
        julienne_vegetables
        cut_french_fries
        cut_waffle_pattern
        shave_delicate_produce

6. Sauce whisk
    Name: Sauce whisk
