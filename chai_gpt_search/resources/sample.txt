Idea for a schema - 
    Tool name
    Synonyms (options)
    Description
    Image
    actions attrs - Determined by schema design.


Examples -
1. Tourné knife.
    Name: Tourné knife
    Description:
        Tournée is French word for "turned". It is used to refer to a special type of vegetable cut which results in a 7 sided cut. Some consider to be a type of paring knife.
    Image: TBD
    actions:
        peel_round_produce
        extract_stem_core
        remove_surface_blemish
        sculpt_decorative_shape

2. Paring knife.
    Name: Paring knife.
    Description:
        A small, short-bladed knife (usually 3-4 inches) with a straight or slightly convex edge. It is the most versatile tool for precision tasks that require control, usable both in the hand (peeling) and on a cutting board (mincing).
    Image: TBD
    actions:
        peel_round_produce,
        peel_irregular_produce,
        extract_stem_core,
        slice_small_produce,
        mince_small_aromatics,
        trim_meat_fat,
        pierce_check_texture,
        sever_binding_material

3. Slicer knife.
    Name: Slicer knife
    Description:    
        Long blade with a round or pointed tip; blade may be flexible or rigid, tapered or round, or have a fluted edge.
    Image: TBD
    actions:
        carve_cooked_protein
        slice_cured_fish
        slice_uniform_portions
        portion_boneless_meat

4. Boning knife.
    Name: Boning knife
    Description:    
        Blade is thinner than that of chefs knife, about 6 inches long, and rigid. It is used for separating raw meat from bone.
    Image: TBD

        debone_raw_protein
